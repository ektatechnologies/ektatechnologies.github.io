Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all games published by Ekta Technologies, including Meowdoku, Merge Drop 2048, Purrfect Pour, Sudoku, Shikaku, Connect Dot, Block Blast Mania and Arrow Escape (collectively, “our Games”).

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the game and features you use, information may include device and app information, advertising identifiers, gameplay and saved-game data, purchase verification details, and diagnostic or performance data. The exact data depends on the services and SDKs included in each game.

2. How We Use Information

We use relevant information to operate the games, provide gameplay and requested features, save and synchronize progress, verify purchases, show and measure ads where enabled, improve stability, prevent abuse, and respond to support requests.

3. Google AdMob (Advertising)

Our games use Google AdMob to serve advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, advertising IDs, IP address, ad interactions and diagnostic information. Consent choices are presented and applied where required. See Google's advertising policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases)

Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store full payment card details. Our games may receive purchase tokens, product identifiers and purchase status to validate transactions and provide purchased digital items or features.

5. Google Play Games Services

When you choose to use Google Play Games features, Google may process your Play Games profile, player identifiers, achievements and related game information to provide sign-in and game services. Google's terms and privacy policy also apply.

6. Cloud Save (Saved Games)

Our games support Google Play Games Saved Games where available. If you use cloud save, relevant game progress is sent to and stored through Google Play Games Services and may be associated with your Google Play Games account.

7. Analytics, Crash and Performance Data

Analytics and diagnostic tools may process app usage, session events, device and app details, crash reports and performance information to help us understand use and improve reliability. The exact provider and data depend on the SDKs integrated into each game.

8. Data Storage and Security

Information may be stored locally on your device or by the third-party services described here. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Our games are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in a way that violates applicable law. If you believe a child has provided information inappropriately, contact us. Age suitability and advertising treatment may vary by game and region.

10. Your Choices

You can manage privacy and advertising choices through in-game consent settings where available, your device settings, Google account settings and Google Play Games settings. You can also uninstall a game to stop using it on your device.

11. Data Deletion

You may contact us to request deletion of information under our control. Some records may remain with Google or other service providers, or be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention or transaction purposes. Saved Games may also be managed through Google Play Games settings where supported.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated date. Please review this page periodically for changes.

13. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact Ekta Technologies at ektatechnologiesofficial@gmail.com.
